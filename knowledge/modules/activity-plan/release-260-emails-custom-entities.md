# Transcript: Extending Activity Plans to Emails and Custom Entities

Target Audience: Administrators

Focus: Multichannel Strategy and Custom Configuration

### 1. Introduction: The Move to Omnichannel

"Welcome. Today, we're looking at how the Activity Plan module is evolving from a visit-centric tool into a true omnichannel command center. Commercial teams need to align every touchpoint—not just face-to-face visits—with their strategic goals. We are now enabling administrators to bring **Life Science Emails** and any **Custom Business Entities** directly into the Activity Plan framework."

### 2. Navigating to Activity Plan Administration

"To begin, we'll navigate to the **Admin Console** and access the **Activity Plan Administration** tab. This is where you define what counts toward a rep's goals. Previously, this was limited to standard visit types, but with the latest release, the 'Activity Object' selection has been significantly expanded."

### 3. Configuring Life Science Emails

"Let's start with a high-priority channel: **Life Science Emails**. By selecting this from the Activity Object list, we can now set specific outreach goals for digital engagement.

Notice how we map the fields to ensure accurate tracking: we can fill in the Type to use the 'Urgency Type' to filter for 'Scheduled' type emails, and map the 'Sent Date Time' and 'Email Status.' This ensures that only successfully sent, strategic emails count toward the rep's actual attainment."

### 4. Unleashing Custom Entities

"Beyond standard channels, every organization has unique workflows. In this demo, we want to track 'Nurse Educator Engagements'—a custom object in our org.

When we select Activity object, we get a new, searchable dropdown. This prevents long, cluttered lists and allows you to quickly find and select your custom entity names or OOTB supported entities. Once selected, you simply map your custom status and account fields. This effectively turns any custom interaction into a trackable activity goal."

### 5. Increased Flexibility: Non-Mandatory Criteria

"We've also introduced a key enhancement to improve efficiency. Previously, 'Activity Type' and 'Value' were mandatory. We heard your feedback that sometimes you just want to track total volume across all types.

You can now leave the **Activity Type Field and Value blank**. This allows the system to aggregate all records for that entity—such as every email or every custom engagement—without needing to segment by a specific subtype, simplifying your configuration process."

### 6. Validation and Activation

"As we save our configuration, the system performs a validation check to ensure the Object API name is valid and that mandatory account and territory mappings are present. Once validated, ensure the **'Is Active'** checkbox is ticked.

Finally, after saving your new Activity Measure Types, remember to run the **Metadata Cache job**. This ensures your new multichannel goals are immediately available to your field reps on both Web and iPad."

### 7. Conclusion

"By extending Activity Plans to include Emails and Custom Entities, you are removing manual workarounds and providing your team with a 360-degree view of their customer engagement strategy. Thanks for watching."
