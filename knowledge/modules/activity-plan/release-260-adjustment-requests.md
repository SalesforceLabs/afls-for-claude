# Transcript: Activity Plan Adjustment Requests

Target Persona: Pharma Sales Reps / Field Users

Focus: Agility and Real-Time Plan Management

### Introduction & Agility Concept

"Welcome to this demonstration of **Activity Plan Adjustment Requests**. Real-world territories are dynamic, and customer activity plans should be too. If users have the required permissions, this feature empowers them to request impromptu changes to their active plans—such as adding new accounts, updating existing goals, or removing accounts that are no longer a priority—ensuring their strategy always matches the reality of their market."

### Navigating to the Feature on iPad

"There are three ways to initiate an adjustment, depending on your current workflow:

- **Accounts Search Screen:** Apply the Activity Plan filter to focus on your current targets. From here, you can swipe left or tap 'Actions' on an account to start the request. This method is best because it automatically populates the account and goal details for you.
- **Activity Metrics Screen:** From your Home Page, view your chart details and select 'Account Summary.' Just like the search screen, using the 'Actions' menu here ensures your data is auto-populated.
- **Account Profile Screen:** If you are visiting a new target, you can initiate a request via 'Quick Actions' in the upper-right corner. Note that starting here pre-fills the account name, but you'll manually select the activity types and products."

### 3. Choosing Your Request Type

"Once you select the **Activity Plan Adjustment Request** action, you'll choose one of three Record Types:

**Option 1: Update Goal Measure:** Use this to adjust a goal for an account already in your plan. If you started from a filtered list, the activity type and products are pre-filled. Note that for updates, you are limited to the products already linked to that goal. Simply enter your new 'Rep Goal' and select a reason."

**Option 2: Set Goal Measure for Additional Activity Types:** Use this to Add a new goal type to an existing account.

**Option 3: Remove Account from Plans** "If an account is no longer viable, select this type to request the removal of all associated goals. You'll just need to check the confirmation box and provide a reason for the removal."

**Option 4: Add Account to Plans** "Use this to bring a new account into your strategy"

### Navigating to the Feature on Web

"There are two primary ways to initiate an adjustment request on web.

First, they can start from the **Activity Plan widget** on their Home Page. By selecting 'View Details' on their chart and navigating to view 'Account Attainment' they get a clear view of their performance metrics, making it easy to spot where a goal update might be necessary."

### Creating the Request

"When you're ready to make a change, select the account and choose the **Activity Plan Adjustment Request** quick action. You'll be prompted to select a Record Type:

- **Update Goal Measure:** To adjust a goal for an account already in their plan.
- **Set Goal Measure for Additional Activity Types:** Add a new goal type to an existing account.
- **Remove Account from Plans:** To retire all goals for a specific account.
- **Add Account to Plans:** To bring in a new account or a suggested target.

Enter the required details and tap **Save**. Your request is immediately submitted for review."

"Once submitted, the rep's request enters a 'Pending' state.

For 'Add Account' requests, the account will appear in their plan only after the request has been fully approved. This ensures their field view remains clean and focused on approved targets only."

Alternatively, the **Accounts Search Screen**. By applying the Activity Plan filter, they can focus on their current targets. They can filter by Activity Type or Product to drill down into specific goals.

### Understanding Governance & Thresholds

"To maintain strategic alignment, administrators may set **Cycle Thresholds**. This limits the total number of changes allowed per plan to ensure focus. Additionally, there are **Review Periods** with specific start and end dates.

It is important to note that if users submit a request after a threshold has been reached or outside of the defined review period, the system will automatically reject the request during the next synchronization. Also, keep in mind that this feature is not available for plans shared to territories or weighted Activity Plans."

### Conclusion

"Activity Plan Adjustment Requests bridge the gap between high-level strategy and field execution, giving sales reps the flexibility to adapt on the go. Thank you for watching."
