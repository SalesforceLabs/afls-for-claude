## Intelligent Content Integration in Web Visit

Hello everyone. In this video, we are focusing on a major enhancement for the Web experience in the Spring '26 release: Intelligent Content Integration in Web Visit.

Intelligent Content Integration brings the Intelligent Content player, previously exclusive to the iPad app, directly to the web browser experience. This ensures that Sales Reps and MSLs have a consistent presentation capability from Visit and track clickstream metrics for content across all devices, so that organizations gain a complete, 360-degree view of content effectiveness across both web and mobile platforms.

**End User Experience Demo**

(Visual: Switch to a Web Browser showing the Dr. Aaron Morita's Account page)

Let's look at how it works through the eyes of Evan, our Sales Rep.

Evan is conducting a visit where he needs to present approved content from his laptop.

He clicks New Visit to open the Visit Engagement for Dr. Morita.

Notice the new 'Content' sidebar menu is available in the left Sidebar.

Evan sees the approved content, such as the 'Immunexis Product Overview'.

When he clicks on + button, the presentation gets added to the visit, so that he can plan the presentation to share in this visit

He can also directly launch the presentation by clicking on the presentation thumbnail

Evan can navigate through slides, sharing the safety profile of Immunexis.

Using the menu, he can switch between presentations or slides within the presentation.

He can directly access the presentations added to the visit in the menu. He also has access to the entire content library with all the presentations he has access to.

After sharing the presentations, he goes back to the visit.

Behind the scenes, the system is recording every interaction—which slides were viewed and for how long. When Evan closes the player, all that data is logged against this specific visit, ensuring that his engagement metrics are complete.

Also, as you can see, the product and message associated with the shared presentation are automatically added to the visit, so Evan does not need to manually enter the data.

**Admin Setup & Configuration**

(Visual: Switch to Lightning App Builder)

Regarding the admin setup, configuration is shared with mobile app and no additional configuration is required for Intelligent Content to work in Visit Engagement page on web. Just like how it works in mobile, if PresentationForum related list is added to the Visit page layout, the Content sidebar menu will be rendered and work.

That is all it takes. The Intelligent Content Player integrated in Web Visit experience will ensure a seamless and compliant presentation workflow for your web users. Thank you for watching.
