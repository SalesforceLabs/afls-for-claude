## Signature Page Snapshot

Hello everyone. In this video, we are going to dive deep into the Signature Page Snapshot feature in the Spring '26 release.

The Signature Page Snapshot is a compliance enhancement that automatically captures a visual image of the entire signature screen—including sample products, quantities, specific disclaimer texts and signature — at the exact moment of sign-off. The system automatically captures the entire screen including the scrolled area and attach the image to the visit record.

(Visual: Switch to iPad screen recording showing the Signature Capture screen)

Now let's see how this looks like in action. We are following Evan, our Field Sales Rep.

Evan has just finished a detailing session with Dr. Morita and has disbursed some samples. He hands the iPad to Dr. Morita for a signature.

(Visual: HCP signing the screen)

Dr. Morita reviews the disclaimer text, checks the product quantities, and signs the screen and pass the ipad back to Evan.

Evan accepts the signature.

Now, let's look at the result from a compliance perspective. If you save the visit and navigate to the Visit Record Page, you see a new image file automatically attached in Files related list.

(Visual: Opening the image file)

When you open it, you see a complete image of the signature page

(Visual: Switch to Salesforce Admin Console)

Configuration is straightfoward and is handled within the Admin Console.

You can just go to the Admin Console. From there, select Visit Administration

Under Signature Settings, you will see 'Capture signature page snapshot' setting.

Simply check this box. Then save.

And make sure you run the cache.

That is all it takes to ensure compliance and audit readiness, bringing operational confidence to the compliance and legal teams.
