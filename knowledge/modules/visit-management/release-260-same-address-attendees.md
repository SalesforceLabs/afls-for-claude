## Show Same Address Attendees in the Sidebar

Hello everyone. In this video, we are looking at a key efficiency update for mobile users in the Spring '26 release. The Show Same Address Attendees feature optimizes the Attendees Sidebar in the Visit Engagement page by defaulting to display the top 5 accounts that share the same physical address as the primary account.

This addresses the inefficiency of searching through broad lists of aligned accounts to find relevant colleagues during group visits, such as Lunch and Learns. By automatically surfacing the people who are physically present at the location, this feature streamlines data entry and allows representatives to log group interactions with maximum speed.

**End User Experience:**

(Visual: Switch to iPad screen recording showing a Visit Engagement page)

Let's see how this improves the workflow for Evan, our Field Sales Rep.

Evan is at a clinic conducting a 'Lunch and Learn' session. He has scheduled the visit with Dr. Morita, but Dr. Abhinav Sinha and Dr. Ashok Kumar are also in the room.

Evan opens the Attendees Sidebar on the Visit Engagement page. (Visual: Tapping the sidebar to expand it)

In the past, he would have to open the Account Search, to search and add additional attendees. But now, look at the list. Under the 'Same Address' section, the system has already identified Dr. Abhinav Sinha and Dr. Ashok Kumar because they are linked to this specific location.

Evan simply taps the plus (+) button next to their names to add them to the visit instantly.

Now let me explain how we configure this feature? This feature relies on your data structure.

The system identifies 'Same Address' attendees by looking at the Contact Point Address object.

It matches accounts that have the same Related Contact Point Address as the primary account for the visit.

This increases the efficiency of the group visit reporting, reducing the administrative burden on the end user, ultimately ensuring accurate attendance capture. Thank you for watching.
