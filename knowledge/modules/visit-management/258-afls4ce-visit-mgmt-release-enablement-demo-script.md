## **Demo Story: A Day in the Life of a Sales Rep**

Persona: Mary Beth, a pharmaceutical sales representative.\
\
Today, we\'ll walk through the end-to-end flow of recording a customer visit in the AFLS for Customer Engagement ipad App using Visit Management features.

We will look at this demo through the lens of Mary Beth, a Pharmaceutical sales representative for the fictitious pharma company Makana Pharma as she goes through her day talking to HCPs about the drug Immunexis.

Mary Beth is visiting Dr. Aaron Morita, a key physician, to discuss new product data, provide samples, and plan future engagement. The visit will be a group call, with Dr. Lydia Jones joining unexpectedly.
