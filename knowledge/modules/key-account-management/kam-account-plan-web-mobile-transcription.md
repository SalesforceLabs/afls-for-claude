### Summary

Vishnu Preethi Gangarapu presented the key account management features within the AFLS, set for general availability in October 2025, which enable brand managers like Alan to plan pre-launch engagement for drugs such as Imunaxis. Vishnu Preethi Gangarapu demonstrated the creation of account plan templates, linking components like goals and action plans, and managing assessment tasks and stakeholder engagement. The presentation also covered the mobile functionality of the AFLS application on an iPad, showcasing accessible account plan management and stakeholder integration.

### Details

- **Key Account Management Features** Vishnu Preethi Gangarapu presented features of key account management, specifically focusing on the account plan functionality in the AFLS, which will be generally available in October 2025. This system helps brand managers like Alan in planning pre-launch engagement for drugs, such as Imunaxis for rheumatoid arthritis, by segmenting target audiences and defining engagement objectives for integrated delivery networks ([[00:00:00]{.underline}](#section)). The key objectives include increasing clinical awareness, formula replacement, and addressing reimbursement delays and denials ([[00:01:31]{.underline}](#section-1)).

- **Account Plan Template Creation** Vishnu Preethi Gangarapu demonstrated the creation of account plan templates, starting with goal definitions, such as a \"pre-launch template for increasing clinical awareness and incorporating into care paths\". These templates must be of type \'affiliate\' to function correctly downstream and have an active status within the library category ([[00:02:39]{.underline}](#section-2)). The templates are structured hierarchically with objectives, action plans (tactics), and assessment tasks (todos), with action plan templates requiring a \'key account management\' type and a target objective to show up in account plans ([[00:03:59]{.underline}](#section-3)).

- **Linking and Importing Account Plan Components** Vishnu Preethi Gangarapu showed how goal definitions can be linked to products and territory objectives, and how action plan templates are assigned to the objective template. This setup prepares the account plan template for sharing with key account managers, who can then import it into their individual account plans ([[00:05:47]{.underline}](#section-5)). When importing, key account managers can select specific objectives, action plans, and tasks, or even create new, custom objectives and action plans tailored to their specific account profiles ([[00:08:12]{.underline}](#section-7)).

- **Account Plan Management and Stakeholder Engagement** Vishnu Preethi Gangarapu highlighted features for managing assessment tasks, including the ability to delete existing tasks, add new ones, assign due dates, and assign tasks to specific users or visits ([[00:09:16]{.underline}](#section-8)) ([[00:12:02]{.underline}](#section-10)). The system also allows for tracking completion statuses of assessment tasks, though the rollup calculation was not demonstrated due to a technical issue ([[00:13:04]{.underline}](#section-11)). Furthermore, Vishnu Preethi Gangarapu explained how to link account plans to multiple products and perform SWAT analysis at both the account and product levels ([[00:14:08]{.underline}](#section-12)).

- **Stakeholder and Participant Management** Vishnu Preethi Gangarapu emphasized the importance of the account plan stakeholders feature, which helps maintain information about key individuals to interact with at a key account ([[00:15:22]{.underline}](#section-13)). Users can add stakeholders from existing HCP/HCO profiles, accounts, or contacts, defining their roles and influence levels within various decision-making chains ([[00:16:26]{.underline}](#section-14)). The system also supports importing stakeholder roles from previous account plans and assigning cross-functional team members as account plan participants ([[00:18:54]{.underline}](#section-16)).

- **Mobile Functionality** Vishnu Preethi Gangarapu provided a demonstration of the AFLS application on an iPad, showcasing how account plans, including SWAT analysis, objectives, products, related analysis, stakeholders, and participants, are accessible and manageable on a mobile device ([[00:21:14]{.underline}](#section-18)) ([[00:26:21]{.underline}](#section-20)). They specifically demonstrated the ability to add multiple accounts to the stakeholder list via a standard search and clarified that the mobile app\'s account access can be configured to only show accounts linked to the user\'s territory ([[00:27:28]{.underline}](#section-21)). The presentation concluded by showing how new objectives and action plan templates, such as those addressing reimbursement issues, can be created and managed directly from the mobile interface ([[00:31:08]{.underline}](#section-24)).
