# SPM Integration

Integration between Salesforce Sales Performance Management (SPM) territory planning and Agentforce for Life Sciences (LSC). Planners define segmentation and territory carving rules in SPM; LSC calculates and publishes account-to-territory assignments on a schedule.

## Winter '27 (Release 264) - Pilot

- [release-264-alignment-rules-processing-engine](./release-264-alignment-rules-processing-engine.md) - Alignment Rules Processing Engine (pilot): ZIP/brick + specialty rules defined in SPM, published to LSC (OT2A / PATI) on a schedule.
